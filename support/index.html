Common questions

How do I log a meal?
Tap Add Meal, then attach a photo, type what you ate, or speak it. Review the draft and tap Save — nothing is logged until you confirm it.

Can I edit a saved meal?
Yes. Open any saved meal and edit it manually or by voice. Every change is kept as a revision, so your history is never lost.

How does the subscription work?
Sanolytica includes a 30-day free trial, then a $30/year subscription. Manage or cancel anytime from your Apple ID subscription settings.

How do I delete my account?
Open the account menu in the app and choose delete account. This permanently removes your meals, revisions, and saved data.
